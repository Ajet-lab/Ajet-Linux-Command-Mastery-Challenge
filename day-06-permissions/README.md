## What I practiced

Today I practiced reading and changing Linux file permissions using `chmod`, understanding permission strings, and working with `umask`. I also practiced applying permissions recursively and checking file ownership and permissions with `stat`.

## What surprised me

What amazed me was understanding the how the octal of the permissions are being generated and what each number stands for... `4 is for read` , `2 is for write` and `1 stands for execute` and I now understand why it's called octal because the max. number we can have is 7.

## What I practiced

Today was the final checkpoint of my 30-Day Linux Command Mastery Challenge.

I practiced building a system health-check script, combining process, service, and log information into a report, connecting to a remote server through SSH, transferring files with `scp`, applying the correct ownership and permissions, scheduling automated checks, parsing logs, cleaning up stale files, and performing a basic security audit.

## What surprised me

What surprised me most was seeing how the commands I learned separately throughout the challenge can actually work together to solve a larger problem.

At the beginning, commands like `ps`, `systemctl`, `journalctl`, `find`, `chmod`, `chown`, and `ssh` felt like completely different topics.

Today's capstone helped me see that they are not isolated commands. They can be combined into a workflow for checking, managing, securing, and automating a Linux system.

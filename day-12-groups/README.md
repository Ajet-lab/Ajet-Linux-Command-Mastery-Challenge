## What I practiced

Today I practiced creating and deleting groups, adding and removing users from groups, checking group membership, and switching my active group. I also explored different ways Linux stores and displays user and group information.

## What surprised me

What I found interesting and suprising was how groups can make access management easier. Instead of having to give every user individually, users can be organized into groups and given access based on their responsibilities.
